# Copy: write like a person

Premium brands sound calm, specific and sure. Write the copy before the layout, and write it the way a sharp human editor would.

## Rules
1. **Short and concrete.** Headlines 3 to 8 words. Say what it is or what it does for someone. "Coats cut to last a decade" beats "Elevate your wardrobe".
2. **Specific over impressive.** Use real details: materials, places, times, prices, numbers with context. "Open Tuesday to Sunday, 6 pm till late" not "An unforgettable dining experience".
3. **Plain punctuation.** Commas and full stops. No em dashes (—), no en dashes as pauses, no double hyphens (`--`), no underscores (`__`), no ellipses for drama, no exclamation marks in headlines. Use a full stop or a comma instead.
4. **No emoji, no sparkles, no symbols as decoration** (✨ 🚀 → •). Middle dots only in a footer line if needed.
5. **Active voice, second person.** "You choose the fabric. We cut it to your size."
6. **Vary sentence length.** Mix a short sentence with a longer one. Do not start three sentences the same way.
7. **No rule of three by reflex.** Do not list three adjectives in every line ("fast, simple and secure").
8. **Buttons say the action:** "Book a table", "Shop coats", "See the work", "Start free trial". Never "Learn more" twice on one page, never "Click here".
9. **Honest numbers only.** If you do not know a real figure, do not invent a statistic. Use a fact the brand can stand behind or leave it out.
10. **Names that sound real.** Products, dishes and projects get proper names ("The Harlow coat", "Grilled sea bream, fennel, lemon"). People in testimonials need a first name, a last initial and a real-sounding context ("Ana R., Lisbon"), or use a press quote with the publication name only if the user provided it.

## Words and phrases to avoid
elevate, unlock, unleash, seamless, seamlessly, effortless, revolutionize, game-changer, cutting-edge, next-level, world-class, best-in-class, state-of-the-art, robust, leverage, empower, harness, delve, embark, journey (for a purchase), realm, tapestry, testament, landscape (non-literal), in today's fast-paced world, look no further, whether you're... or..., not just X but Y, it's not about X it's about Y, take it to the next level, designed with you in mind, crafted with passion, experience the difference, your one-stop shop, dive in, discover the magic, we've got you covered, ever-evolving, holistic, synergy, curated (more than once), bespoke (unless it is truly made to order), premium (do not call yourself premium; show it).

## Page microcopy
- Nav: 3 to 5 plain words ("Shop", "Journal", "Stores", "About").
- Hero subline: one sentence, 12 to 22 words, about the thing itself.
- Section intros: one short sentence. Let images and lists do the rest.
- Forms: labels above fields, helpful placeholders ("you@example.com"), errors that say what to do ("Enter a date after today").
- Empty and error states: say what happened and what to do next, without apologising three times.
- Footer: address or city, contact, a few useful links, legal, a calm sign-off line.

## Final pass (do this every time)
Search your copy for: `—`, `–`, `--`, `__`, `!`, `✨`, "elevate", "seamless", "unlock", "journey", "experience the". Rewrite every hit. Read each headline out loud; if it sounds like an ad, make it plainer.
