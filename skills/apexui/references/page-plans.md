# Page plans

Section order for each kind of site. Every page starts with a nav and ends with a closing call to action and a full footer. Blueprint names refer to `sections.md`. Pick the direction first (`directions.md`).

## Fashion or clothing store (direction: Atelier or Champagne)
1. Nav: logo center, Shop / Collections / Journal left, Search / Account / Bag right (N2)
2. Hero: full-bleed campaign photo with one line of type and "Shop the collection" (H2)
3. New arrivals: 4 to 8 products, image 4:5, name, price (S4 product grid)
4. Story split: atelier photo + 3 short lines about how it is made (S2 split)
5. Category tiles: 2 or 3 large image links ("Coats", "Knitwear", "Shirts") (S5)
6. Quote: one customer line, large serif (S7)
7. Service row: delivery, returns, repairs in plain words, hairline separated (S6 list)
8. Newsletter: one field, one line of value ("Two emails a month, new pieces first") (S10)
9. Footer with oversized wordmark (F2)
Product page: gallery left (sticky on desktop), details right: name, price, size selector, add to bag, fabric and care in an accordion, delivery note.

## Portfolio: photographer, artist, designer (direction: Noir Gallery)
1. Nav: name left, Work / About / Contact right (N1)
2. Hero: type-only statement over a calm background, one line about the practice (H3)
3. Selected work: index list or large alternating images (S8 index list)
4. Full-bleed image band (S3)
5. About: portrait or studio photo + short bio, clients as a plain list (S2)
6. Services or approach: 3 to 4 items with hairlines, no icons (S6)
7. Contact: big email link and availability line (S9 CTA)
8. Footer: minimal (F1)

## Restaurant, cafe, bar (direction: Harbour)
1. Nav with "Book a table" button (N1)
2. Hero: dish or room photo, name, one line, booking button (H1 split or H2)
3. Opening hours and address, very visible (S6 list)
4. Menu: printed card style, 2 columns, dish, short description, price (S11 menu)
5. Story: the kitchen, the producers (S2)
6. Gallery: 3 to 5 photos in an uneven grid (S5)
7. Private dining or events (S2 reversed)
8. Booking CTA with phone number (S9)
9. Footer with map link and hours (F1)

## Architecture, interiors, hotel (direction: Stone and Linen)
Nav (N1), hero full-bleed (H2), intro statement (S1), projects alternating splits (S2 x3), approach list (S6), press or awards as plain text list (S6), contact (S9), footer (F2).

## SaaS, software, fintech (direction: Swiss Precision or Midnight Ink)
1. Nav with "Sign in" link and "Start free" button (N1)
2. Hero: headline, subline, two buttons, product screenshot in a thin frame (H1)
3. Logos of real customers only if provided; otherwise skip (S12)
4. Problem and outcome statement (S1)
5. Three to five features as a hairline list with a screenshot per feature (S2 alternating)
6. How it works in 3 steps (S6 numbered)
7. Quote (S7)
8. Pricing: 2 or 3 plans, flat, one highlighted with a border (S13)
9. FAQ (S14)
10. CTA (S9) and footer (F1)

## AI product (direction: Midnight Ink or Swiss Precision)
Same as SaaS, plus a live demo block (input and a real example output) right after the hero, and a plain "What happens to your data" section before pricing.

## Personal brand, coach, consultant (direction: Champagne or Stone and Linen)
Nav, hero with portrait (H1), what I help with (S6), method (S2), results with real names only if given (S7), offer and price (S13), FAQ (S14), booking CTA (S9), footer.

## Event or conference (direction: Bold Editorial)
Nav, hero with date and city huge (H3), speakers grid (S4 adapted), schedule by day in tabs (S6), venue (S2), tickets (S13), FAQ (S14), CTA (S9), footer (F2).

## Mobile app landing (direction to match the app)
Nav, hero with a phone showing the real home screen (H1), three screens with one line each (S2 alternating), privacy statement (S1), quote (S7), download buttons (S9), footer (F1). App screens themselves: see `mobile-app.md`.
